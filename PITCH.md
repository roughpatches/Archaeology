# Pitch

- **Fantasy:** you are an archaeologist sent to a mysterious island to uncover its secrets, one
  expedition at a time.
- **Core loop:** prepare in the overworld (gather, explore, build up camp, pack supplies), go on an
  expedition into a procedurally generated area, come back with finds, learn more about the island,
  prepare for a deeper or stranger expedition.
- **Core verb:** the expedition (D23). Each floor asks whether to dig what you've found here or push
  deeper while your stamina and supplies last. Dig sites are found along the way.
- **Tone:** wondrous and adventurous.
- **Look:** top-down pixel art in the cozy farming-sim tradition (Stardew Valley's view, all art
  original).
- **Scope cap (proof of concept):** base camp with Professor Philius, a small overworld around camp
  with three gatherables, one expedition area of about 5 generated floors plus a fixed chamber at
  the bottom, about 12 finds, two camp buildings to build, the supply boat, save and load.
- **Out of scope (proof of concept):** fighting (D21), a second expedition area, pets, NPC
  friendship meters, weather, multiplayer, mobile, a browser build.

Full design: the Claude Doc "Island expeditions: rescoped design".
