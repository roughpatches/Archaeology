# Pitch

- **Fantasy:** you are a field archaeologist in a world of dead empires and extinct beasts, digging
  up what they left and working out how each one rose and why it fell.
- **Core verb:** the dig. Uncovering a find in a trench whose contents and odds were shaped by the
  survey and site map you brought to it. If the dig isn't satisfying, nothing around it saves the
  game.
- **Tone:** wondrous and adventurous. Every site is a door into a lost world.
- **Look:** top-down pixel art in the tradition of cozy farming sims such as Stardew Valley, with a
  day clock and seasons. All art original.
- **Scope cap (proof of concept):** one zone, one civilization told through its first chapter, one
  fossil bed, about 12 finds, two museums, one collector, a camp that can keep, donate and sell, two
  tool upgrades, a 12-minute day, and two finds that only appear at a certain time or season.
- **Out of scope (proof of concept):** combat, skill levels, idle or offline progress, crafting
  beyond conservation, weather, multiplayer, mobile, NPC romance or friendship meters, a second zone.
