## Build: gray-box dig v1, 9 Nov 2026

What I played: the first gray-box (trowel and brush, one find, three map grades), as a published
artifact.

Read — was it clear what was happening? Yes: "feels and makes sense as a prototype for the actual
digging."

Decide — did I face a real choice? Trowel versus brush works. Wants more: two more tools.

Feedback — did good vs bad outcomes feel different? Uncovering the artifact should feel more
unique, involved and exciting.

Where it felt flat: one find per dig, and the dig is the whole process. Wants digs to hold several
finds, some of them pieces of a large artifact assembled over time, and a full process of
scouting, surveying and excavating that can take a player several days per site.

Bugs/visual issues: none reported.

Verdict: PASS for the core verb, with REWORK asked for (dig v2: D13 to D16).
